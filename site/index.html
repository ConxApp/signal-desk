How to read this

Score (0–100)
How unusual, broad, fresh and unpriced the attention is. 60+ is worth a look; 40–60 is “watch”; below 40 is background.
σ (sigma)
How far above a name’s own 45-day normal today is. 2σ is notable, 3σ+ is a genuine spike. Every name is judged against itself, so a giant and a micro-brand are comparable.
Stage
↗ rising = still accelerating · ▲ peaking = near its recent high · ↘ fading = coming off a peak · quiet = nothing unusual.
Unpriced / late
Unpriced = attention is high but the stock is flat on normal volume (the setup). Late = the stock already ran 15%+ in 5 days.
Finance noticed
The ticker is already trending on Yahoo Finance — the investing crowd is looking, so the information edge is smaller.
Past spikes
What the stock did in the 10 trading days after this name’s previous attention spikes. Small samples — treat as context, not a forecast.
Channels
Wikipedia lookups (how many people are googling it), news volume (GDELT), Reddit consumer chatter (when enabled), App Store rank, Google trending searches.

Research tool, not advice. A spike in attention is a hypothesis about demand, not a forecast of revenue or price.
